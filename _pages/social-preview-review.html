Every distinct social-preview image on the site, with the pages using it. Images shared by more than one page come first.

This walks the same chain the theme uses to choose og:image — header.og_image, then header.overlay_image, then header.image, then header.teaser, then the site-wide default — so what is below is what a link preview actually gets. Dimensions and the aspect flag are measured in your browser from the real file, not read from the filename. The target is 1200×630 (1.91:1).

A page marked via overlay_image has no card of its own and is falling back to its on-page banner. One marked via site default sets no image at all and is showing the house card.
